Research

Training-Free Speculative Decoding & Token Reuse

Aug 2026 – Present

Advisor: Prof. Jiacheng Shen

Optimized the draft-proposal path for training-free LLM inference. Multi-source suffix retrieval, trie-based candidate fusion, and confidence-calibrated, cost-aware selection reduced decode-phase wall time by 20.4% against the original Suffix proposer on Llama 3.1-8B with an NVIDIA A40, across five full benchmark runs.

Reworked sequential per-request speculation into batched, cross-request parallel proposal with a persistent C++ thread pool and GIL release. At batch size 16, CPU proposal time fell from 280.6 to 102.9 μs (2.73× speedup) with bitwise-identical drafts.

MEMOIR: Runtime Acceleration for Mobile GUI Agents

Mar – Aug 2026

Advisor: Prof. Jiacheng Shen

Co-designed a compact reduction DSL and confidence-gated memory retrieval to reuse successful GUI reasoning and avoid repeated vision-language inference. On AndroidControl with Qwen3-VL 8B, per-step latency fell from 3,314 to 1,019 ms (3.25× speedup), while low-level step accuracy improved from 65.2% to 69.3% over a chain-of-thought agent. Manuscript submitted to ICLR.

PACESatMAE: Distributed Satellite Pretraining

Mar 2025 – May 2026

Advisor: Prof. Zuchuan Li

Built a distributed PyTorch training and streaming data pipeline for terabyte-scale satellite imagery, focusing on multi-GPU throughput and I/O efficiency. Developed 256-level spectral tokenization with learnable embeddings for MAE pretraining on 170+ band PACE hyperspectral imagery. Downstream chlorophyll-a estimation reached R² = 0.986, with approximately 2.5× faster convergence from MAE pretraining.
