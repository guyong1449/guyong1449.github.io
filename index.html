Research

Training-Free Speculative Decoding & Token Reuse

Aug 2026 – Present

Improving suffix-tree-based draft generation through multi-source retrieval, trie-based candidate fusion, and confidence-calibrated, cost-aware selection. The approach reduced decode-phase wall time by 20.4% against the original Suffix proposer on Llama 3.1-8B with an NVIDIA A40, across five full benchmark runs.

I also developed batched, cross-request parallel draft proposal using a persistent C++ thread pool and GIL release, achieving a 2.73× CPU speedup at batch size 16 while preserving bitwise-identical drafts.

MEMOIR: Runtime Acceleration for Mobile GUI Agents

Mar – Aug 2026

Co-designed reusable reasoning recipes and confidence-gated memory retrieval to avoid repeated vision-language inference. On AndroidControl with Qwen3-VL 8B, MEMOIR achieved a 3.25× per-step speedup and improved low-level step accuracy from 65.2% to 69.3% over a chain-of-thought agent. Manuscript submitted to ICLR.

PACE SatMAE: Satellite Self-Supervised Pretraining

Mar 2025 – May 2026

Built a distributed PyTorch training pipeline for terabyte-scale satellite data and developed spectral tokenization for masked autoencoder pretraining on 170+ band PACE hyperspectral imagery. Downstream chlorophyll-a estimation reached R² = 0.986, with approximately 2.5× faster convergence from MAE pretraining.
